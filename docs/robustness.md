# Robustness, uncertainty and action sensitivity (Week 7)

`twist robustness --config experiments/robustness.yaml` (or
`sbatch scripts/slurm/multiworld.sbatch experiments/robustness.yaml robustness`) runs, per world and
classifier:
- a **base** run: all explainers and seeds;
- one **shift** run per pre-declared shift;
- a **perturb** run, for stability.

It then writes `artifacts/robustness/<name>/report/`.

## Shift library (`twist.worlds.shifts.apply_shift`)

A shifted run trains the classifier, fits estimated mechanisms, and gives explainers their
reference data and oracle knowledge all on the **base** world. Factual units come from the
**shifted deployment world**, which is also the replay oracle. This is the realistic situation:
explanations computed with yesterday's knowledge, enacted in today's world.

| Level | Kinds | Amount means |
|---|---|---|
| innovation | `gaussian`, `uniform`, `laplace` (variance-matched law), `heteroscedastic`, `correlated` | h added / cross-channel correlation |
| mechanism | `coefficients`, `delete_edges`, `spurious_edges`, `nonlinearity`, `delay` | misspecification level (see `mechanism_access.md`) |
| observation | `noise`, `mcar`, `saturating` | sensor noise std, or saturation, × state std / missing rate; the world becomes partially observed |
| decision | `threshold`, `site`, `retrain` | threshold += amount × label-score std / label site at amount·T (refit) / evaluation classifier retrained with another seed |

Shifts that do not apply to a world (edge edits on nonlinear worlds) are skipped with a logged
reason. Every evaluated population is saved with its world, so shifted runs pass the audit.

## Explanation stability (`twist.worlds.shifts.perturb_units`, `metrics.robustness.stability_pairs`)

Each factual unit's innovations are perturbed by `perturb_scale` × channel std (0.05), and the
unit is regenerated by the world. The result is a nearby, *coherent* unit, not off-manifold noise.
The same units are explained again with the same seeds. Per method, the paired records give:

| Metric | Meaning |
|---|---|
| onset | same action start time |
| variables | Jaccard overlap of intervened variables |
| cost | 1 − relative difference of action magnitude |
| outcome | same world outcome after replay |
| target | the proposal reaches (or misses) the target in both |
| **action stability** | mean of onset, variables and outcome |

## Tolerance sensitivity (`metrics.sensitivity`)

The change predicate `|x̂ − x| > τ` drives `t0`, sparsity, the action support and `D`. Saved
proposals are re-scored at `τ ∈ {1e-5, …, 1e-1} × channel std`: hard Pearl CF-faith, `D`, and the
unchanged-entry fraction. *Ranking stability* is the Kendall τ between the method ranking by `D` at
each τ and at the reference τ = 1e-3. Only exact worlds are profiled.

## Uncertainty

| Quantity | Definition |
|---|---|
| CIs on every headline metric | hierarchical bootstrap: resample worlds, then units within worlds (`metrics.aggregation.hierarchical_bootstrap`), 95% percentile |
| `p_robust` | for an ensemble of plausible mechanisms `{M_j}` (the world plus 7 copies with strengths perturbed by 0.25 × spread), the fraction under which the action's unit-level replay reaches the target: `p_robust = mean_j 1{ℓ^{M_j}(x^A) = y*}` |
| interventional target probability | `P(ℓ*(X^A) = y* | do(A), prefix)` from 16 fresh-noise rollouts in the true world (`evaluation.interventional_samples`) |
| reported probability & calibration | action-native explainers report `P(h(X^A) = y*)` under *their* mechanism (`uncertainty.p_target_model`); the report gives the mean gap to the true interventional probability and the ECE against realized outcomes |
| explanation-set variability | per unit, mean pairwise normalized L1 between proposals of different seeds |

## Brittleness verdict

A method is **model-valid** when A ≥ 0.8. It is **model-valid but brittle under mechanism
uncertainty** when, in addition, the **95% CI** of some world-success drop lies entirely above 0.10.
The drops considered are:
- under a mechanism shift, `C_base − C_shift`, estimated with a world-blocked bootstrap (worlds
  resampled jointly, units independently, because base and shifted units differ);
- under the ensemble, `C − p_robust`, paired per unit.

Week 7 used the point estimate. Its 30-unit shifted runs have CI half-widths of 0.15–0.3, so the
rule became CI-aware in Week 8 (decision log, Week 7 item 10). Replay-error sensitivity is the largest increase
in median replay error under a mechanism shift: low < 0.01 ≤ moderate < 0.05 ≤ high.

## Acceptance checks (pre-declared)

1. Every headline metric (A, B, C, feasible) of every (method, condition) row with scorable units
   has a finite CI.
2. Every method has a tolerance-sensitivity profile.
3. The brittleness verdict is computed for every method and discriminates: it flags at least one
   method and clears at least one.

## Report contents

- the check table;
- world success under every shift, with CIs, plus a heatmap;
- a summary table: A, C, `p_robust`, largest drop with its 95% CI, action stability, replay-error sensitivity,
  calibration gap, seed variability, verdict;
- tolerance profiles and ranking stability;
- one robustness card per method, in the plan's format.

## Not yet covered

Oracle-mechanism *posterior* abduction on partially observed worlds is still not implemented:
oracle explainers are evaluated on exact worlds, and on observation-shifted worlds they abduct
with the base (exact) assumption. The observation shifts therefore measure the cost of assuming
exact observations, not a posterior method.
